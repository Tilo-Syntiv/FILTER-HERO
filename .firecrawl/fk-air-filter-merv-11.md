## What does MERV 11 protect against?

MERV 11 air filters are a step up above MERV 8 filters, capturing even finer airborne particles and irritants. They are designed to capture particles between 1 and 3 microns, providing additional comfort and protection against:


Dust mites

Captures microscopic mites and waste that are prevalent in bedding and can contribute to growing allergy issues in the home.

Pollen

Traps pollen allergens that can contribute to allergies.

Pet dander

Protects against tiny flakes of skin from pets that can linger in the air.

Lint and fabric fibers

Traps lint and fabric fibers shed by clothing, bedding, and upholstery.

Mold spores

Captures airborne mold spores.

Fine dust

Eliminates fine dust that settles on surfaces and can irritate noses and sensitive lungs.

Smoke particles

Filters and captures smoke particles so they don't cling to surfaces.

Smog and fine particles

Eliminates smog from traffic and nearby construction activities.

If your system is not designed for higher-efficiency filters, stepping down to a [MERV 8](https://filterking.com/air-filter/merv-8) or [MERV 8 Carbon](https://filterking.com/air-filter/merv-8-carbon) filter may be ideal. If you are seeking an even more efficient solution, [MERV 13](https://filterking.com/air-filter/merv-13) filters may be right for you, depending on your household's activity, foot traffic, and current location with regard to the environment.


## Who is MERV 11 best for?

The MERV 11 air filters are designed for homes with allergies and in some cases, asthma. Those with respiratory illnesses or compromised immune systems may benefit from MERV 11 air filters. These filters are significantly more powerful than alternative MERV filters at capturing smaller particles, such as MERV 8.

## How to find your air filter size?

Check the label on your current filter or use a tape measure to get the exact length, width, and depth before selecting your size.

![nominal vs actual air filter size](https://filterking.com/img/product/p-img-custom-filters-find-size.webp)

### ![Nominal Size](https://filterking.com/img/product/p-icon-nominal-size.svg)  Nominal size

**Nominal size** is what you normally see printed on the label. This is usually
a rounded
size which is often **not the true size** of your filter.


### ![Actual Size](https://filterking.com/img/product/p-icon-actual-size.svg)  Actual size

**Actual size is the true size** of the air filter, usually written in smaller
font below the
nominal size.


### Measuring

Measure your current air filter or the length, width, and depth of the opening slot to get
the actual size. Round it up to the nearest whole inch to get the nominal size.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Will MERV 11 completely stop my allergies?

Not completely. However, the filters can significantly reduce common triggers. MERV 11 captures many airborne particles and allergens such as pet dander, pollen, mold spores, and dust mite debris. However, it's important to combine complementary source control activities with your filter to truly minimize potential allergens from making their way into your home.

### How often should I replace MERV 11 filters if I have pets?

Most homes with pets should replace a MERV 11 air filter every 6–8 weeks. However, if you have multiple pets in the home or if you're experiencing excessive shedding, a replacement once every 4–6 weeks may be necessary.

### Is MERV 11 too restrictive for my older furnace?

It depends on your existing system. Many older systems can use MERV 11 without issues, but some struggle with the increased airflow resistance. Warning signs of incompatibility include temperature inconsistencies, longer furnace run times, and strange noises coming from your furnace.

### Can I use MERV 11 in spring and MERV 8 in winter to save money?

Yes. This is a common and often effective strategy, especially if you understand your system's needs and household demands. When pollen and dust are at their worst, using MERV 11 is best. A MERV 8 filter during the winter can work as outdoor allergens dissipate and the airflow demands increase.

### Does MERV 11 work for asthma, or do I need MERV 13?

MERV 11 provides strong filtration for many asthma-related triggers, especially from pollen, pet dander, and dust. However, for capture of even finer particles, [MERV 13 filters](https://filterking.com/air-filter/merv-13) may be a better option (if your system is built for high efficiency). Using MERV 11 filters alongside room purifiers can be an excellent solution as well.

### Why does my air quality seem worse after switching to MERV 11?

There are a few possible reasons, such as the filter is not fitting tightly, allowing air to bypass the media. Your system may be struggling with restricted airflow, reducing overall air movement. Your filter may be clogging faster than expected in homes with pets, shedding, dust, and pollen. Dust may already be settled on surfaces that have yet to be removed by a previous HVAC filter. Always check for proper sizing and inspect your filter for early clogging to monitor your system's performance year-round.
