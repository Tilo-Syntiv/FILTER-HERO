## How to find your air filter size?

Check the label on your current filter or use a tape measure to get the exact length, width, and
depth before
selecting your size.

![nominal vs actual air filter size](https://filterking.com/img/product/p-img-custom-filters-find-size.webp)

### ![Nominal Size](https://filterking.com/img/product/p-icon-nominal-size.svg)  Nominal size

**Nominal size** is what you normally see printed on the label. This is usually
a rounded
size which is often **not the true size** of your filter.


### ![Actual Size](https://filterking.com/img/product/p-icon-actual-size.svg)  Actual size

**Actual size is the true size** of the air filter, usually written in smaller
font below the
nominal size.


### Measuring

Measure your current air filter or the length, width, and depth of the opening slot to get
the actual size. Round it up to the nearest whole inch to get the nominal size.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Will a 20x25x4 MERV 13 filter restrict airflow in my system?

No, 20x25x4 MERV 13 filters are designed with deeper pleats for proper airflow. A 4-inch depth captures particles with an expanded surface area without sacrificing airflow.


### Do I need special housing for 20x25x4 air filters?

Yes, your HVAC unit must accommodate 20x25x4 air filters with a proper return air rack and media cabinet. Always verify filter slot size and depth before purchasing a 20x25x4 replacement air filter.


### How do I know when my 20x25x4 filter needs replacement?

If you notice reduced airflow, increased dust collection, and higher energy bills, it may be time for a 20x25x4 filter replacement. Most 20x25x4 air filters last between 6 and 12 months.


### Are 20x25x4 filters harder to install than thinner filters?

No, the installation of 20x25x4 filters is straightforward and typically installed in the same way as 1-inch air filters. Always check that airflow arrows (printed on the filter's frame) are facing towards the blower. Proper handling (without forcing or bending the filter) prevents damage and inefficiency.


### Can I fold or bend a 20x25x4 filter to make it fit?

No, folding or bending a 20x25x4 air filter can damage the pleats and frame. This can result in reduced efficiency and airflow. Using the correct size for your HVAC system is essential.


### Can I vacuum my 20x25x4 filter to extend its life?

No, it is not recommended to vacuum your 20x25x4 filter when it is clogged with particles and dust. However, a light vacuuming may temporarily allow your filter to last as you await a new 20x25x4 air filter replacement.


### Will a 20x25x4 filter improve my energy bills?

Yes, a new 20x25x4 filter will reduce your HVAC unit's strain while simultaneously improving airflow and efficiency. The more efficient your HVAC system's air filter, the more efficient your unit will be.
