## Frequently Asked Questions

## How do I choose the right delivery frequency?

Your delivery frequency should be based on how often you need to change filters and how many filters stock up with each delivery - ordering larger filter packs will help lower the price per filter. You can also use our [change frequency quiz](https://filterking.com/change-frequency-quiz) if you're not sure how frequently you should be changing your air filters.

## Can I change my delivery frequency after signing up?

Yes, you can change your delivery frequency at any time.

## Can I cancel my subscription at any time?

Yes you can pause or cancel air filter subscription service at any time. There is no minimum subscription period for our air filter auto-delivery service.

## Does the discount apply to every order?

Yes, the 5% discount applies to the first and all future orders you receive using our auto-delivery service.

## Is there a minimum order value or commitment?

No, you can set up auto-delivery for as few or many filters as you want.

## Can I subscribe to any filter size?

Yes, simply add any size you need to your order and enable auto-delivery in the product page or during checkout to enable subscription for that air filter.

## Is shipping free on subscription orders?

Yes, shipping is free on all website and subscription orders.

## What happens if I have too many filters?

If you haven't run out before auto-delivery is due, you can pause your subscription and reenable it later.
